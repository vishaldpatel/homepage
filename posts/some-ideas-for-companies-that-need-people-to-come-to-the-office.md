# Some ideas for companies that need people to come to the office
## Published: 2026-03-19

Most companies that allow work from home provide a generous stipend to help people create an ideal home office.

But, you want people to come to the office and you want the best of the best because you're the best and you deserve the best! Here are a few incentives, other than your ambitious mission, the incredible team and your amazing personality:

- Free meals worth eating, especially breakfast, to get them in and firing on all cylinders.
- Free gas / transportation for up to 10 miles, in exchange for clocking in before office hours begin.
- Get HR to help people find housing closer to the office! With AI taking away so much of the boring parts of their job, their role finally becomes about the Human part of Human Resources!
- Hire a high touch concierge service to help your employees get chores done! Help your employees have even more time to pursue the things they love when they are not working.

I'm not a very creative person. And, I have a feeling that if I used an AI to write this, then it would come up with even better ideas ;).
